# 🚴 India Bike Buyers Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **India Bike Buyers Analysis Dashboard** is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel to analyse customer demographics and bike purchasing behaviour across different regions of India.

The main purpose of this project is to understand customer purchasing patterns and identify the factors that may influence a customer's decision to purchase a bike.

The project involves working with raw customer data, performing data cleaning and transformation, creating PivotTables, analysing different customer segments, and developing an interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard provides a visual representation of important business information, helping users understand customer behaviour and identify potential customer segments for marketing and business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- To analyse customer demographics such as age, gender, income, education, and occupation.
- To understand bike purchasing behaviour across different customer segments.
- To identify the relationship between customer income and bike purchasing decisions.
- To analyse customer preferences based on commute distance.
- To compare purchasing patterns across different age groups.
- To create an interactive dashboard for better data visualisation and business insights.
- To support data-driven marketing and customer-targeting decisions.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses an India Bike Buyers dataset containing **1,026 customer records and 14 fields in the prepared working sheet**.

The dataset provides information about customers, including their demographic characteristics, financial background, lifestyle, and bike purchasing decisions.

### Dataset Attributes

| Column Name | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | Unique customer identification field |
| Marital Status | Customer's marital status |
| Gender | Male or Female |
| Income | Annual customer income |
| Children | Number of children |
| Education | Customer's education level |
| Occupation | Customer's professional category |
| Home Owner | Whether the customer owns a home |
| Cars | Number of cars owned |
| Commute Distance | Distance travelled by the customer |
| Region | Customer's Indian state or region |
| Age | Customer's age |
| Age Brackets | Categorised age groups |
| Purchased Bike | Indicates whether the customer purchased a bike |

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project was completed through the following stages:

### 1. Data Collection
Collected the India Bike Buyers dataset containing customer demographic and purchasing information.

### 2. Data Cleaning
Reviewed the dataset and prepared the information for analysis.

- Converted abbreviated marital status values into readable categories.
- Converted gender abbreviations into Female and Male.
- Reviewed column headings and data formats.
- Prepared the dataset for further analysis.

### 3. Data Transformation
Created an additional column called **Age Brackets** to categorise customers into different age groups.

The age categories used in the working sheet are:

- Adolescent
- Middle Age
- Old

This transformation helps analyse customer purchasing behaviour across different age segments.

### 4. Data Analysis
Created PivotTables to summarise customer information and compare purchasing patterns.

### 5. Dashboard Development
Created an interactive dashboard using Excel PivotCharts and slicers to present important findings in a visually understandable format.

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Average Income by Gender and Bike Purchase

Analysed the average income of male and female customers based on whether they purchased a bike.

This analysis helps understand income differences between purchasing and non-purchasing customer segments.

### 2. Bike Purchases by Commute Distance

Examined the number of customers who purchased and did not purchase bikes across different commute-distance categories.

The analysis helps identify whether daily travel distance is associated with bike purchasing behaviour.

### 3. Bike Purchases by Age Brackets

Categorised customers into different age groups and compared their bike purchasing decisions.

The prepared dataset contains:

- Adolescent: 112 customers
- Middle Age: 719 customers
- Old: 195 customers

This helps identify the age segments that may offer opportunities for targeted marketing.

### 4. Bike Purchases by Age

Analysed bike purchasing behaviour across individual customer ages to understand differences in purchasing patterns.

## 🔍 Key Insights from Data Analysis

Based on the saved PivotTable summaries:

1. **Age Group Analysis:** Middle Age represents the largest customer segment, with 719 records in the prepared dataset.

2. **Commute Distance:** The saved commute-distance PivotTable shows 38 bike purchases among customers travelling 0–1 miles, compared with 4 purchases among customers travelling more than 10 miles.

3. **Purchase Summary:** The saved purchase summaries contain 71 Yes and 66 No records.

4. **Gender and Income:** The average income comparison shows differences between male and female customers across bike purchase categories.

5. **Customer Segmentation:** Age, income, gender, and commute distance provide useful dimensions for understanding customer behaviour.

These insights can help businesses understand customer segments and develop more focused marketing strategies.

*Note: The purchase totals above refer to the saved PivotTable summaries, not the full 1,026-row source dataset.*
